VST3 / AudioUnit effect that emulates the sound of DAT option 2 (LP mode): 12-bit non-linear PCM (RFC3190 / IEC 61119) with a 32 kHz anti-alias filter and 50/15 us emphasis.

--- Controls ---

Input Gain
  Level going into the quantizer. Decides
  how hard the 12-bit non-linear stage is
  driven.

Threshold
  Where the non-linear table starts to
  compress. Drag to the very top for the
  authentic DAT LP setting (512 LSB).

32kHz
  Anti-alias filter of the 32 kHz sample
  rate.

Emphasis
  50/15 us pre-emphasis and de-emphasis.

Output Gain
  Makeup level after the quantizer.

Bypass
  Passes the input through untouched.

--- Input Meter ---

LP mode takes the 16-bit linear input and builds a 12-bit code out of it. Samples under the threshold (511 and below by default) pass straight through; above that the quantizer step doubles every octave, so the louder the sample the coarser the step.

The segment colours show that step size at each level. White is pass through, nothing dropped. Pale yellow through orange is 1 to 6 bits dropped, which covers the whole standard table at threshold 512. Lowering the threshold pushes the top regions past 6 bits, and those run orange to red.
